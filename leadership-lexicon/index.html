      How to play
      • Every clue is a definition. Type the leadership term it describes. Numbers in brackets give letter counts, e.g. (7,3) is two words, (5-2) is hyphenated. Spaces and hyphens are not entered.
      • Click or tap a square to select it; tap again (or press Space) to switch between Across and Down.
      • Arrow keys move, Tab / Shift+Tab jump between clues, Enter checks the current word.
      • Each correct letter is worth 10 points. A context hint halves that word's points; revealed letters score nothing.
      • Correct words lock in green and show a usage note. Finish to get a study sheet of every term.
